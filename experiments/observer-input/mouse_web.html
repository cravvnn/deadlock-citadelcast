2 · Run one short mouse move

Starting from your current view, the helper sends a fixed horizontal move over two seconds at 120 requested updates/s. It sends 240 relative mouse units in total. The angle depends on your sensitivity; this is not a calibrated 12° pan.

Focus Deadlock during the countdown, close its console/menu, then keep your physical mouse still and release WASD. A final process check follows the countdown before movement starts.

Hold Escape, press WASD or a mouse button, or Alt-Tab to stop future events. Physical mouse motion alone does not trigger Stop and can mix with the test. No keys or buttons are held by the helper; no automatic return move is sent.

Evidence and limits

A successful Windows insertion does not prove the game consumed it. This is an acceptance/smoothness experiment, with no live quality or Valve risk guarantee. No game hooks, memory access, console commands, capture or game launch. Windows settings and game sensitivity remain unchanged.
